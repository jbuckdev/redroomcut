RedroomCut

RedroomCut is a small gaming-clips media brand. We cut short, vertical highlight clips from gaming and streaming moments and publish them on our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok channels, with credit to the original creator on every clip.

ClipEngine, our publishing tool

ClipEngine is the software we built to run this. It finds a moment, cuts it into a captioned vertical clip, and queues it for review. A person approves each clip before it is posted. Posting is done through the official APIs of YouTube (Data API), Instagram (Graph API) and TikTok (Content Posting API), only to channels that RedroomCut owns and has signed in itself.

What we do and do not do

  • We post only to accounts we own. We never post to, or read from, anyone else's account.
  • We credit and tag the original creator, and take any clip down within 24 hours of a request.
  • We add no watermarks or promotional links to creators' content.
  • We do not sell data, and we hold none about the public. See the Privacy Policy.
