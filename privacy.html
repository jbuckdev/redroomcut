Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This policy covers the RedroomCut website and the ClipEngine publishing tool ("the Service"), operated by James Buchan (RedroomCut).

Who uses the Service

The Service is used by RedroomCut itself, to publish to accounts RedroomCut owns. It has no public sign-up and no other users. When we say "you" below we mean the owner of a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account who signs that account in to the tool, which today is RedroomCut.

What we collect

  • Account sign-in data. When an account is connected, the platform gives the tool an access token and a refresh token, plus the account's public identity (channel name or username, account ID, avatar and display name). We use TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload; Instagram content-publishing permissions; and the YouTube scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.
  • Post records. Which clip was posted to which account, when, and the platform's response (post ID, status, errors).
  • We do not collect data about viewers, followers, commenters or any member of the public, and the website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule the clips RedroomCut chooses to publish, to show the owner which account is connected, and to report errors. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or training of models.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on a private computer owned by RedroomCut, in a local file readable only by the operator. They are never published, never put in source control and never written to logs. The tool's pages are not reachable from the public internet.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data. It is sent only to the platform it came from (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok) to carry out a post the owner requested.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while an account is connected and deleted when it is disconnected in the tool. You can also revoke access at any time: Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Instagram (Settings, Apps and websites), TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). Post records are kept for our own audit trail. To ask for deletion of anything we hold, write to jbuchan.dev@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

Platform terms

Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of TikTok and Instagram is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Children

The Service is not directed at children and collects no data from them.

Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Questions: jbuchan.dev@gmail.com.
